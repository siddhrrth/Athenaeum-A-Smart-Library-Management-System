Reservation Policy & Guidelines

Enforced Rule

Reservation Criteria: Only books that are currently unavailable (checked out, issued, or reserved by another patron) can be reserved. If a copy is already available in the library stacks, it must be borrowed directly through circulation.

Queue Priority Requests are processed strictly First-Come, First-Served (FCFS) upon return.
7-Day Hold Window Reserved copies remain on hold for 7 days post-check-in before auto-expiring.
Cancellation Permitted Staff and patrons can cancel any pending reservation before physical pickup.
