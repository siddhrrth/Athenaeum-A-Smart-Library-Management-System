Overdue Fee Structure & Regulations

₹5.00 / day

Standard overdue fines accumulate at ₹5 per calendar day beyond the designated due date. Patrons with more than ₹150 in unpaid fines are automatically blocked from checking out or reserving additional materials until full clearance.

Accepted: Cash, UPI, Card Instant receipt generated upon settlement Waive requests require Chief Librarian approval
